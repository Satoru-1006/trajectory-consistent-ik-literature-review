## What the abstract says

For non-cuspidal serial robots, the abstract describes partitioning joint space into Uniqueness Domains (UDs), separated by singularity surfaces, with an IK branch associated with each UD. When the task-space trajectory requires a transition between UDs, the method crosses a singular configuration and aims to preserve continuity and differentiability of the joint variables. The abstract says the approach was evaluated in simulation and experimentally against competing methods and reports advantages, but gives no robot configuration details, comparator names, sample counts, numerical metrics, or constraint guarantees.
