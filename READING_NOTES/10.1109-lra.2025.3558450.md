# Full-text reading note — BoundPlanner: A Convex-Set-Based Approach to Bounded Manipulator Trajectory Planning

## Bibliographic identity and source

- Thies Oelerich, Christian Hartl-Nesic, F. Beck, and Andreas Kugi, IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (2025), DOI [10.1109/LRA.2025.3558450](https://doi.org/10.1109/lra.2025.3558450).
- Read arXiv author manuscript [arXiv:2502.13286v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13286), which identifies the RA-L manuscript and 2025 acceptance history. The later arXiv revision was not compared line by line with the Version of Record.

## Research question and assumptions

Plan bounded, collision-free Cartesian routes quickly and generate a joint-space trajectory with online replanning in changing environments. The tested fixed-base 7-DOF platform is a KUKA LBR iiwa.

## IK representation

BoundPlanner builds convex collision-free workspace sets (ellipsoid or convex-hull constructions), links intersections into a Cartesian guide path, and combines it with BoundMPC for bounded joint-space trajectories and replanning. It does not enumerate or label IK branches.

## Solution-branch treatment

No explicit branch identity, IK solution-set graph, branch continuation, or branch transition method is provided. The authors explicitly state that a joint trajectory along the bounded Cartesian path is not guaranteed to exist and needs further investigation.

## Singularity handling

No singularity detector, avoidance condition, or crossing/transition method is identified. Kinematic reachability is handled by the bounded planning/control formulation, but the stated lack of guaranteed joint-trajectory existence limits that interpretation.

## Constraints and executable feasibility

The path planner constructs obstacle-free convex regions; BoundMPC constrains a finite set of selected arm control points using sphere-expanded obstacles and bounded path sets. This is useful model-based collision avoidance, but the selected-point/obstacle model and sampled numerical planning are not a signed-distance continuous-clearance certificate. Joint kinematic limits are included by BoundMPC; do not infer branch feasibility or guaranteed joint lift from the Cartesian corridor alone.

## Guarantees

The convex-set path stays within constructed obstacle-free sets. The authors expressly do not guarantee existence of a joint trajectory along every such path. No theorem establishes branch continuity or singularity transition under joint/collision constraints.

## Experiments and reported results

Experiments use a KUKA LBR iiwa 14 R820 in reaching and moving-shelf replanning scenarios. In eight box-placement trials, BoundPlanner reports mean planning time 0.11 s and zero failures; a set of 20 replans reports about 0.1 s average (Secs. IV–V, Table I and experiment figures). These are planner results, not proof of continuous clearance.

## Authors' limitations

The explicit joint-trajectory-existence caveat is central. The conclusion identifies embedding the planner in more complex online settings and improving high-level path choices as future work.

## Evidence-bounded TCIK classification

Adjacent evidence for bounded Cartesian collision-free planning plus joint-space constrained MPC and hardware replanning. It does not establish branch × singularity treatment; joint-trajectory existence along the bounded path remains open.
