## What can be said from the abstract only

The abstract presents a DH-based test for whether a revolute serial robot admits closed-form IK, representing solvable robots through three algebraic subproblems. It then describes general closed-form algorithms that use explicit formula solutions to determine joint angles and claims applicability to low-degree-of-freedom serial robots. It does not describe a path-level IK method, persistent branch labels, trajectory-continuity criteria, singularity-transition logic, or hard joint-limit guarantees.
